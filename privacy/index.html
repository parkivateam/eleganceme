EleganceMe Privacy Policy

See it on you.

Last updated: September 27, 2026

EleganceMe respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the EleganceMe mobile application, why it is processed, and the choices available to you.

1. About EleganceMe

EleganceMe is a mobile application that provides AI-powered virtual try-on features and photo filters. Users can provide a photo of a person and a clothing image to generate an AI preview showing the clothing on that person.

AI-generated previews are simulations. Fit, size, fabric behaviour, colours, appearance and fine details may differ from reality.

2. Photos and AI Try-On

To provide the AI Try-On feature, EleganceMe processes the person photo and garment photo selected by the user.

These images may be transmitted securely to our backend infrastructure and to an AI service provider solely for the purpose of generating the requested result.

EleganceMe's backend is designed to process try-on images without permanently storing them in an application image database. Images are transmitted for processing and the generated result is returned to the application.

Users must confirm that they have permission to use the images they submit.

3. Photo Filters

EleganceMe includes photo filters that can be applied to images selected by the user. Filter processing is separate from the AI Try-On feature.

4. Anonymous Installation Identifier

EleganceMe may generate an anonymous installation identifier for an app installation. This identifier may be used for service operation, abuse prevention, rate limiting, credit management and security.

The installation identifier is not intended to directly identify the user's real-world identity.

5. Advertising

EleganceMe uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements and interstitial advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location and information about interactions with advertisements, subject to applicable law and the user's consent choices.

Where required, EleganceMe uses Google's consent management tools to request or manage advertising and privacy choices.

For more information about how Google uses information from apps that use its services, please visit Google's privacy documentation.

6. Rewarded Advertising

EleganceMe may allow users to voluntarily watch a rewarded advertisement in exchange for access to an AI Try-On.

A reward is granted only when the rewarded advertisement has been completed according to the applicable advertising system.

7. Purchases

EleganceMe may offer digital credits and other digital products through Google Play Billing.

Payment information is handled by Google Play. EleganceMe does not receive or store full payment-card details.

Purchase information or purchase tokens may be processed when necessary to verify purchases, prevent fraud and provide purchased features.

8. Third-Party Services

EleganceMe may use third-party services necessary to operate the app, including:

  • Google Play services and Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform
  • Supabase for backend infrastructure
  • AI processing providers for virtual try-on generation

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable data-protection requirements.

9. Sharing Product Links

Users may share product links or images from other applications to EleganceMe. When a product link is submitted, the link may be processed by our backend to attempt to identify an appropriate product image.

10. Reports and Safety

Users may report generated results or other issues. Information included in a report may be processed for troubleshooting, abuse prevention, safety and service improvement.

11. Data Retention

EleganceMe aims to retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, legal obligations, security and fraud prevention.

AI Try-On images processed through EleganceMe's backend are not intended to be permanently stored by the EleganceMe backend. Third-party service providers may apply their own limited processing or retention practices as described in their applicable terms and privacy policies.

12. International Processing

EleganceMe is intended for users in multiple countries. Information may therefore be processed in countries other than the user's country of residence where our service providers operate.

Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for international data transfers.

13. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing, and rights relating to consent.

Advertising privacy choices may also be available through the consent and privacy controls presented within the application.

14. Children's Privacy

EleganceMe is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed toward children.

15. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information processed through EleganceMe. However, no internet transmission or electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when EleganceMe's features, service providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
